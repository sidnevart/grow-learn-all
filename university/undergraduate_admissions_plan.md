## Тесты

### SAT / ACT

**Статус:** Большинство школ test-optional после COVID, но для international students с non-US diploma — **strongly recommended**.

| Тест | Формат | Баллы | Хороший результат | Отличный результат |
|------|--------|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| SAT | Digital, adaptive | 400-1600 | 1400+ | 1500+ |
| ACT | Paper/digital | 1-36 | 32+ | 34+ |

**Когда сдавать:**
- First attempt: August or October 2026
- Second attempt (если нужно): November or December 2026
- Регистрация: collegeboard.org (SAT), act.org (ACT)

**Где сдавать в России:**
- SAT: Не проводится в России с 2022. Нужно ехать: Istanbul, Tbilisi, Yerevan, Almaty, Belgrade, Warsaw
- ACT: Может быть доступен в некоторых международных школах. Проверить на act.org

**Совет:** Если есть выбор — SAT. Больше тестовых центров, более predictable.

### English Proficiency

| Тест | Формат | Хороший результат | Отличный | Стоимость | Notes |
|------|--------|-----------------|---------|-----------|-------|
| TOEFL iBT | Online | 90+ | 100+ | ~$200 | Золотой стандарт |
| IELTS Academic | Paper/computer | 6.5+ | 7.5+ | ~$200 | UK предпочитает |
| Duolingo English Test | Online | 115+ | 130+ | ~$50 | Принимают ~4000 школ, быстрый |

**Совет:** Начать с Duolingo как diagnostic. Если 130+ — может хватить для многих школ. Если < 120 — готовиться к TOEFL/IELTS.

**Когда сдавать:**
- First attempt: June-July 2026
- Final attempt: September-October 2026 (before applications)

## Визы

### United States — F-1 Student Visa

**Текущий статус для российских паспортов:**
- Не запрещено получать F-1 visa
- НО: US Embassy в Москве прекратило routine visa services в 2022
- Нужно подавать в third-country embassies

**Third-country options (проверить availability):**

| Город | Страна | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Almaty | Казахстан | Часто самый доступный |
| Yerevan | Армения | Много русскоязычных |
| Tbilisi | Грузия | Близко, но slots могут быть ограничены |
| Istanbul | Турция | Большое консульство, но busy |
| Belgrade | Сербия | Не требует visa для россиян |
| Warsaw | Польша | Schengen required |
| Dubai | UAE | Дорого, но slots есть |

**Required documents:**
1. Form I-20 from university (proof of admission + SEVIS fee payment of $350)
2. DS-160 application (online)
3. Passport valid 6+ months beyond program end
4. Photo (US visa specification)
5. Proof of financial support (bank statements, scholarship letters — showing $60K-80K/year)
6. Proof of ties to home country (property, family, job offer)
7. Transcripts + test scores

**Interview tips:**
- Be ready to explain why this specific university
- Be ready to explain why you will return home (or have plans)
- Do NOT mention sensitive defense/AI topics
- For MIT/Stanford — research interests will be scrutinized
- Schedule interview IMMEDIATELY after accepting offer (slots fill up)

### Europe — Student Visas

#### Germany
- **Visum zu Studienzwecken** (Student visa)
- Blocked account (Sperrkonto): ~11,208€ per year
- Can open via Fintiba or Expatrio from abroad
- Apply at German embassy in Moscow OR consulate in nearby country

#### Netherlands
- **MVV (entry visa)** + residence permit
- University sponsors the process
- Proof of funds: ~13,000€/year
- Apply through university after acceptance

#### UK
- **Student Route visa**
- CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) from university
- Proof of funds: tuition + living costs (~£9,207/year London, ~£7,098 outside)
- IHS (Immigration Health Surcharge) — healthcare fee
- Can apply 6 months before program start

#### Ireland
- Student visa
- Proof of funds: ~€10,000/year
- Apply through INIS (Irish Naturalisation and Immigration Service)

## Платформы

### Common Application (Common App)
- **Сколько школ:** ~900 (mostly private, many public)
- **Стоимость:** $50-90 per school (fee waivers available for internationals with need)
- **Main essay:** 650 words (1 essay for all schools)
- **Supplemental essays:** Per school (varies)
- **Early Decision/Action deadline:** November 1
- **Regular Decision deadline:** January 1-15
- **Ссылка:** commonapp.org

### Coalition Application
- **Сколько школ:** ~150
- **Similar to Common App**, but with "locker" feature for early portfolio building
- **Ссылка:** coalitionforcollegeaccess.org

### UC Application (University of California)
- **Сколько школ:** All UC campuses (Berkeley, UCLA, UCSD, etc.)
- **Deadline:** November 30 (no early round!)
- **Essays:** 4 of 8 "Personal Insight Questions" (350 words each)
- **No letters of recommendation**
- **Very limited aid for internationals**
- **Ссылка:** universityofcalifornia.edu/apply

### ApplyTexas
- **Сколько школ:** UT Austin, Texas A&M, etc.
- **Deadline:** December 1 (priority)
- **Ссылка:** applytexas.org

### UCAS (UK)
- **Сколько школ:** Up to 5 UK universities
- **Personal Statement:** 1 essay (4,000 characters) for all 5 schools
- **Oxford/Cambridge/Medicine deadline:** October 15
- **Other deadline:** January 31
- **Ссылка:** ucas.com

### Uni-Assist (Germany)
- **Centralized processing for German universities**
- **Fee:** €75 for first application, €30 for each additional
- **Winter semester deadline:** July 15
- **Summer semester deadline:** January 15
- **Ссылка:** uni-assist.de

### Studielink (Netherlands)
- **Centralized for Dutch universities**
- **Deadline:** Varies (usually May 1 for fall intake)
- **Ссылка:** studielink.nl

## Эссе

### Common App Main Essay (650 words)

**Prompt (choose 1 of 7):**
1. Some students have a background, identity, interest, or talent that is so meaningful...
2. The lessons we take from obstacles...
3. Reflect on a time when you questioned or challenged a belief...
4. Reflect on something that someone has done for you...
5. Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization...
6. Describe a topic, idea, or concept you find so engaging...
7. Share an essay on any topic of your choice.

**Recommended approach:** Prompt #6 (intellectual curiosity) or Prompt #1 (background/identity as engineer).

**Outline:**
- Hook: The moment you realized your ClickHouse query was taking 45 minutes and you decided to fix it
- Body: How you taught yourself distributed systems, built the migration, measured 5x improvement
- Reflection: What you learned about yourself as engineer and learner
- Future: What you want to study and why this university

### UCAS Personal Statement (4,000 characters)

**Structure:**
- 20% — Why CS/Engineering (personal story)
- 40% — Academic preparation (courses, self-study, projects)
- 30% — Practical experience (work, internships, open-source)
- 10% — Extracurriculars and why this field

### Supplemental Essays

**Common prompts:**
- "Why this university?" → Mention specific professors, labs, courses
- "Why this major?" → Connect to your work experience
- "Describe a community you belong to" → Open-source community, Telegram dev community
- "What do you want to accomplish in 10 years?" → Be ambitious but realistic

## Бюджетная стратегия (если финансы ограничены)

### Сценарий A: Минимальные затраты

1. **Germany (TU Munich/Saarland)** — ~300€/semester tuition
2. **Blocked account:** ~11,208€/year = ~$12K/year
3. **Living costs:** ~800-1000€/month
4. **Total:** ~$20K-25K/year
5. **Funding:** DAAD scholarship + part-time work (20 hours/week allowed)

### Сценарий B: Средние затраты

1. **Netherlands (TU Delft/UvA)** — ~12-15K€/year tuition
2. **Living costs:** ~1000-1200€/month
3. **Total:** ~$30K-35K/year
4. **Funding:** Holland Scholarship + talent scholarships + part-time work

### Сценарий C: Community College -> Transfer (US)

1. **Year 1-2:** Community College (e.g., Foothill, De Anza, Santa Monica) — ~$10K/year
2. **Year 3-4:** Transfer to UC Berkeley, UCLA, UIUC — ~$40K/year
3. **Total average:** ~$25K/year
4. **Pros:** Save money first 2 years, same diploma
5. **Cons:** No "prestige" campus experience first 2 years

### Сценарий D: Full-ride scholarship pursuit (US)

1. Apply only to schools with full-ride merit scholarships
2. ASU (Presidential Scholarship), BU (Trustee), UIUC (Stamps)
3. Requires near-perfect stats + exceptional essays
4. High risk, high reward
