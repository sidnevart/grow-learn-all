# Robertson Scholars Leadership Program

> **Тип:** Full-ride merit scholarship (joint Duke + UNC)
> **Университеты:** Duke University (US News #7) + UNC Chapel Hill (US News #22)
> **Уровень:** Бакалавриат
> **Покрытие:** Full tuition + fees + housing + meal plan + books + travel + research/enrichment fund
> **Количество:** ~18 students/year
> **Язык:** Английский

---

## Обзор программы

Robertson Scholars — уникальная программа: студенты одновременно зачислены в Duke и UNC Chapel Hill (10 miles apart). Можно:
- Брать курсы в обоих вузах
- Жить в обоих кампусах (rotate each semester)
- Использовать ресурсы обоих (libraries, labs, professors)

### Покрытие (full-ride):
- Full tuition (обоих вузов)
- Housing
- Meal plan
- Books
- Travel (3 domestic trips + 1 international)
- Research/enrichment fund ($10,000+)
- Laptop
- Summer funding for research/internship

---

## Кого они ищут

### Robertson ищет:

1. **Leadership with impact (30%)**
   - Initiating change (не просто participating)
   - Evidence of making things better
   - Scalable impact

2. **Intellectual curiosity (25%)**
   - Love of learning
   - Interdisciplinary interests
   - Asking hard questions

3. **Character / Integrity (20%)**
   - Ethical decision-making
   - Resilience
   - Authenticity

4. **Community engagement (15%)**
   - Service with depth
   - Understanding systemic issues
   - Commitment to improving community

5. **Collaborative spirit (10%)**
   - Work well with others
   - Build teams
   - Share credit

### Для твоего профиля

**Robertson ценит:**
- Unusual paths (ты — не typical 18-year-old applicant)
- Real-world impact ($600K+ business impact)
- Leadership in professional setting
- Self-directed learning (MIT OCW, Harvard CS50)
- International perspective

**Как подать:**
- Frame work at T-Bank as "professional leadership in technical setting"
- Open-source as "building global developer community"
- ClickHouse migration as "initiating organizational change"
- Self-study as "intellectual curiosity outside formal structure"

## Как выиграть

### Шаг 1: Подать на Duke или UNC (один Common App)

**Важно:** Robertson nomination automatic при подаче на Duke или UNC. Но можно и nominated separately.

**Application:**
- Duke: Common App or Coalition
- UNC: Common App
- Must indicate interest in Robertson on application

### Шаг 2: Написать Duke/UNC essays + Robertson essays

**Duke Essays (examples):**

*"Please share with us why you consider Duke a good match for you."*
```
"Duke's CS department emphasizes systems and infrastructure — 
exactly the gap between my production experience and theoretical 
knowledge. Professor Owen Astrachan's work on CS education aligns 
with my belief that the best engineers understand both theory 
and practice.

The Robertson program's cross-campus access is uniquely valuable 
for me. I'll combine Duke's systems courses with UNC's entrepreneurship 
resources (through 1789 Venture Lab) to build infrastructure 
for AI startups.

My work at T-Bank showed me that the best solutions come from 
combining perspectives — backend engineers, data scientists, 
product managers. Duke + UNC = the academic equivalent."
```

**Robertson-specific Essays (if nominated):**

*"Describe a time when you identified a problem and took initiative to solve it."*
```
"When our analytics team accepted 45-minute query times as 'normal 
for big data,' I saw a problem others didn't. Over three weekends, 
I researched ClickHouse partitioning, built a prototype, and 
presented it to skeptical stakeholders.

The migration saved $600K+ annually and made our critical scenario 
5x faster. But the real lesson was organizational: I learned to 
build consensus, document decisions, and mentor others through 
technical change.

This experience taught me that leadership isn't having the answer 
— it's convincing others to look for better questions. That's 
what I bring to the Robertson community."
```

### Шаг 3: Пройти semi-finalist round

**If nominated as semi-finalist (~100 students):**
- Submit additional materials
- Write deeper essays on leadership + community
- Provide detailed activity descriptions

### Шаг 4: Finalist interview

**If finalist (~40 students):**
- Weekend interview event at Duke/UNC
- Group activities
- Individual interviews
- Panel discussions

**Interview prep:**
- "Why Duke + UNC?" → "Two perspectives, one goal"
- "How will you use both campuses?" → "Duke for systems theory, UNC for social impact of technology"
- "Tell us about a failure" → ClickHouse migration setbacks, learning
- "What community will you build?" → "Bridge international students and local tech community"

## Процесс подачи

1. **Apply to Duke or UNC through Common App**
   - Check "Robertson Scholars interest" box
   - Complete all essays

2. **Automatic nomination review**
   - Admissions committee nominates strong candidates
   - Or self-nominate through Robertson website

3. **Semi-finalist round**
   - Submit additional essays
   - Provide recommendation (may be additional letter)

4. **Finalist weekend**
   - Travel to Durham/Chapel Hill (paid by program)
   - Interviews, group activities

5. **Final selection**
   - ~18 selected from ~40 finalists
   - Results in April

---

## Советы для российских граждан

1. **Two universities = two opportunities:** Even if not selected for Robertson, you're still admitted to Duke or UNC.
2. **Research Triangle Park:** Duke + UNC located in RTP — major tech hub. Mention interest in internships there.
3. **International perspective valued:** Robertson seeks diverse scholars. Your Russian background + engineering experience = unique.
4. **Travel component:** Scholarship includes international trip. Mention where you'd want to go and why (e.g., "Germany to study their engineering education system").
5. **Community building:** Robertson emphasizes building community between campuses. Show you've done this before (open-source community, team building).
