# Estonian Government Scholarship

> **Тип:** Government scholarship (Estonian Ministry of Education)
> **Уровень:** Бакалавриат, Магистратура, PhD
> **Покрытие:** Monthly stipend + tuition waiver + health insurance
> **Количество:** ~100 students/year globally
> **Язык:** Английский

---

## Обзор программы

Estonia — digital powerhouse Европы. Skype, Wise (TransferWise), Bolt — все из Эстонии. Страна активно привлекает tech-студентов.

### Варианты стипендий:

1. **Estonian Government Scholarship (degree studies)**
   - Monthly stipend: €350-500
   - Tuition waiver (fully funded)
   - Health insurance
   - Для бакалавриата, магистратуры, PhD

2. **DoRa scholarships** (for research)
   - Для research stays
   - Не для full degree

3. **University-specific scholarships**
   - University of Tartu: various merit scholarships
   - TalTech: technology-focused scholarships

### Почему Estonia для твоего профиля:
- Digital-first society (e-Residency, digital government)
- Strong tech ecosystem (startup density highest in Europe)
- English-taught programs
- Low cost of living (~€600-800/месяц)
- 20 hours/week work allowed
- Path to EU residency

## Кого они ищут

### Estonian Government Scholarship:

1. **Academic excellence (35%)**
   - Strong grades
   - Technical preparation

2. **Motivation (25%)**
   - Why Estonia specifically
   - How education aligns with goals

3. **Fit with Estonian values (20%)**
   - Innovation, digital mindset
   - Entrepreneurship
   - Open-source mindset

4. **Future plans (20%)**
   - How you'll use education
   - Connection to Estonia/Europe

### Для твоего профиля

**Ты ideal candidate:**
- Backend engineer — Estonia needs software engineers
- Open-source contributor — aligns with Estonian digital culture
- AI experience — Estonia invests in AI
- Entrepreneurial (Svobodno, freelance) — Estonian startup mindset

**Как подать:**
- "I want to study in Estonia because it's the most digital society in the world"
- "My experience building production systems aligns with Estonia's approach to government as a service"
- "After graduation, I plan to contribute to Estonia's tech ecosystem"

## Как выиграть

### Шаг 1: Выбрать программу

**Университеты:**

| Университет | Программа | Tuition (без стипендии) | Стипендии |
|-------------|-----------|------------------------|----------|
| University of Tartu | Computer Science BSc | €6,000/год | Government + university |
| TalTech (Tallinn Tech) | IT Systems Development | €6,000/год | Government + university |
| University of Tartu | Data Science BSc | €6,000/год | Government + university |
| TalTech | Cybersecurity BSc | €6,000/год | Government + university |

**Application:**
- University of Tartu: apply via DreamApply portal
- TalTech: apply via university portal

### Шаг 2: Подготовить application

**Documents:**

1. **Motivation letter**
```
"Estonia represents the future I want to build. As a backend 
engineer, I've seen how legacy systems slow down innovation. 
Estonia rebuilt its entire government as digital-first — from 
tax filing to voting. This is the approach I want to learn.

My experience at T-Bank taught me scale: 30M transactions/day, 
$600K+ impact, 5x speedups. But I need theoretical foundation 
to build systems that don't just work — they're resilient, 
observable, and maintainable.

University of Tartu's Computer Science program emphasizes 
algorithmic thinking and system design. Estonia's startup 
ecosystem provides real-world context. After graduation, I 
plan to work on distributed systems that make digital 
services accessible globally — starting with the lessons 
I'll learn in Tartu."
```

2. **CV**
- Highlight technical projects
- Mention open-source
- Include metrics ($600K+ impact)

3. **Transcript**
- School + university
- Translated

4. **Recommendation letters**
- 2 letters (academic + professional)

5. **Proof of English proficiency**
- IELTS 6.0+ / TOEFL 80+ / Duolingo 110+

### Шаг 3: Подать через university portal

**University of Tartu (DreamApply):**
1. Go to [dreamapply.com](https://dreamapply.com) → University of Tartu
2. Create account
3. Select program
4. Fill personal information
5. Upload documents
6. Write motivation letter
7. Pay application fee (~€50-100)
8. Submit

### Шаг 4: Отдельно подать на Estonian Government Scholarship

**After university acceptance:**
1. Go to [archimedes.ee](https://archimedes.ee) (Archimedes Foundation manages scholarships)
2. Find scholarship application
3. Fill form
4. Upload acceptance letter + documents
5. Submit

## Процесс подачи

1. **Apply to university**
   - Select program at Tartu or TalTech
   - Submit application + documents
   - Pay fee

2. **Wait for acceptance**
   - Usually May-June

3. **Apply for Estonian Government Scholarship**
   - Through Archimedes Foundation
   - Submit acceptance letter + financial docs

4. **Wait for scholarship results**
   - July

5. **Apply for visa / residence permit**
   - D-visa (long-term)
   - Temporary residence permit for study

6. **Arrive in Estonia**
   - September
   - Register with university
   - Apply for Estonian ID card

## Работа во время учебы

**Estonia позволяет:**
- 20 hours/week during semester
- Full-time during breaks
- No work permit needed for students

**Возможности:**
- IT internships (Skype, Wise, Bolt ecosystem)
- Startup jobs (Estonia has 7 unicorns per million people)
- Remote work for EU companies

**Зарплаты:**
- Junior developer: €1,500-2,500/month
- Part-time (20 hrs): €800-1,200/month

## Советы для российских граждан

1. **Visa:** D-visa (long-term) для studies. Apply через Estonian embassy (check where — may be Moscow or neighboring country).
2. **E-Residency:** Consider applying for Estonian e-Residency (digital ID for business). Allows running EU company remotely. €100.
3. **Russian language:** Many Estonians speak Russian. Helps with daily life.
4. **Cost:** Even with stipend, need €200-500/month extra. Plan part-time work.
5. **EU pathway:** After graduation — 1 year job search visa. Can lead to EU permanent residency.
6. **Tech community:** Active Russian-speaking developer community in Tallinn.
