# Trustee Scholarship — Boston University

> **Тип:** Merit-based full-tuition scholarship
> **Университет:** Boston University (US News #41)
> **Уровень:** Бакалавриат
> **Покрытие:** Full tuition ($60,000+/год) for 4 years
> **Количество:** ~20 students/year
> **Язык:** Английский

---

## Обзов программы

Trustee Scholarship — самая престижная merit scholarship Boston University. Покрывает полную стоимость обучения (не housing).

### Что включает:
- Full tuition (все 4 года)
- Honors College membership
- Priority registration
- Special events with university leadership
- Alumni network

### Что НЕ включает:
- Housing (~$15,000/год)
- Meal plan (~$6,000/год)
- Books and supplies

---

## Кого они ищут

### Trustee Scholarship ищет:

1. **Top 1% academic achievement**
   - Near-perfect GPA
   - SAT 1500+ / ACT 33+ (if submitted)
   - Rigorous coursework

2. **Intellectual curiosity**
   - Love of learning beyond grades
   - Self-directed projects
   - Research interest

3. **Community impact**
   - Service with depth (not just hours)
   - Leadership in initiatives
   - Social entrepreneurship

4. **Diversity of thought**
   - Unique perspectives
   - International background valued
   - Different life experiences

### Для твоего профиля

**Boston University ценит:**
- International diversity (BU ~25% international students)
- Urban engagement (BU is in Boston — city campus)
- Interdisciplinary thinking
- Professional experience (BU has strong co-op program)

**Твои сильные стороны:**
- Russian passport + professional experience = unique perspective
- Built real products (Svobodno, ARC, ProofForge)
- $600K+ business impact shows results
- Self-taught advanced CS while working full-time

## Как выиграть

### Шаг 1: Подать на BU через Early Decision

**Важно:** Trustee Scholarship consideration automatic при подаче Early Decision или Early Action. Regular Decision applicants eligible, но fewer slots.

**BU Deadlines:**
- Early Decision 1: November 1
- Early Decision 2: January 4
- Regular Decision: January 4

**Trustee Scholarship deadline:** Same as application deadline.

### Шаг 2: Написать Trustee Scholarship essay

**BU Trustee Essay Prompt (2025):**
"Please discuss with specificity why you wish to attend Boston University and what you hope to accomplish as a member of the Boston University community. (600 words)"

**How to write it (structure):**

```
Paragraph 1: Hook — Why BU specifically
"Boston University's location at the intersection of academia and 
industry mirrors how I've learned: by building. In Moscow, I built 
systems serving millions of users. At BU, I want to understand 
the theoretical foundations those systems rest on."

Paragraph 2: Academic fit — Specific programs/professors
"BU's Computer Science department emphasizes systems and 
infrastructure — exactly what I need. Professor [Name]'s work on 
distributed databases aligns with my ClickHouse migration 
experience. The CS 330 (Algorithms) and CS 460 (Database Systems) 
curricula fill gaps in my self-taught knowledge."

Paragraph 3: Professional experience as preparation
"My 3 years at T-Bank taught me what production systems really need: 
reliability, observability, and graceful degradation. I bring this 
practical perspective to classroom discussions. When we study 
consensus algorithms in CS 350, I'll contribute real-world examples 
from Kafka and ClickHouse deployments."

Paragraph 4: BU's co-op program
"BU's co-op program is uniquely valuable for me. Unlike traditional 
internships, co-ops integrate with coursework. I plan to co-op at 
a Boston fintech or AI infrastructure company, applying 
classroom theory to real systems while contributing industry 
experience back to the classroom."

Paragraph 5: Community contribution
"As a Trustee Scholar, I'll contribute by organizing hackathons 
for social good, mentoring first-year international students, and 
starting an open-source club that bridges BU research with 
real-world applications."

Paragraph 6: Future vision
"After BU, I plan to lead infrastructure teams building AI systems 
at scale — combining my Russian engineering experience with 
American theoretical training. Trustee Scholarship isn't just 
financial support; it's an investment in cross-cultural engineering 
excellence."
```

### Шаг 3: Highlight BU-specific fit

**Research BU before writing:**
- Check BU CS faculty research interests
- Look up specific courses (CS 330, CS 350, CS 460)
- Learn about BU Spark (student innovation lab)
- Understand BU's "Hub" curriculum (interdisciplinary requirements)
- Check BU's international student support

**BU-specific facts to mention:**
- BU is in Boston — tech hub (HubSpot, Wayfair, Boston Dynamics)
- BU has strong industry connections
- BU's "Hub" requires interdisciplinary courses — perfect for engineer with diverse interests
- BU Study Abroad programs (can combine with internship)

### Шаг 4: Ace the academics

**Academic requirements:**
- GPA: Top of class (в России — аттестат с отличием)
- SAT: Submit if 1500+ (BU test-optional, но Trustee competitive)
- Course rigor: AP/IB preferred (если available)

**If no AP/IB:**
- Emphasize self-study: "Completed MIT OCW Algorithms, Harvard CS50, freeCodeCamp Java/Spring while working full-time"
- Show academic initiative

## Процесс подачи

1. **Apply to BU through Common App**
   - Select Early Decision or Regular Decision
   - Pay application fee ($80)

2. **Submit Trustee Scholarship Essay**
   - Upload in Common App or BU portal
   - Must be submitted BY application deadline

3. **Automatic consideration**
   - No separate application for Trustee
   - All applicants automatically considered

4. **Wait for decision**
   - ED1: Mid-December
   - ED2/Regular: Late March

5. **If awarded:**
   - Trustee Scholarship comes with admission letter
   - Accept by May 1

---

## Советы для российских граждан

1. **Early Decision advantage:** BU admits ~50% of class ED. Trustee slots prioritized for ED.
2. **International diversity:** BU actively seeks international Scholars. Emphasize your background.
3. **Boston location:** Mention interest in Boston tech scene (startups, research labs).
4. **CSS Profile:** Even with Trustee (full tuition), file CSS Profile if housing costs are concern.
5. **Interview:** BU offers optional interviews for internationals — take it.
