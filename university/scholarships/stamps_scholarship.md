# Stamps Scholarship — полная стипендия для талантливых студентов

> **Тип:** Merit scholarship (funded by Stamps Family Charitable Foundation)  
> **Уровень:** Бакалавриат  
> **Покрытие:** Full tuition + fees + housing + meal plan + books + laptop + enrichment fund ($20K+)  
> **Язык:** Английский

## Кого они ищут

Stamps Scholarship ищет **top 1% applicants** по комбинации:

### 1. Academic Excellence (30%)
- Near-perfect GPA (в России — 4.8+ или все 5ки)
- SAT 1550+ или ACT 35+ (если submitted)
- Rigorous coursework (AP, IB, honors — если available)

### 2. Leadership (25%)
- Student government, club president, team captain
- Initiating projects (не просто участие)
- Impact on community

### 3. Character / Perseverance (20%)
- Overcoming obstacles
- Unique background
- Work ethic demonstrated through actions

### 4. Service / Community Engagement (15%)
- Volunteer work with measurable impact
- Mentoring others
- Social entrepreneurship

### 5. Innovation / Creativity (10%)
- Research projects
- Artistic achievements
- Entrepreneurial ventures

### Для твоего профиля
**Ты сильно отличаешься от typical Stamps applicant:**
- Типичный applicant: straight-A student, president of 3 clubs, 100 hours volunteering
- Ты: production engineer with $600K+ business impact, open-source maintainer, built real products

**Как подать это:**
- Frame work experience as "professional leadership"
- ARC и ProofForge — это innovation + service (open-source для community)
- AI code review bot — это innovation in practice

## Как выиграть

### Шаг 1: Подать на вуз через Early Action

**Важно:** Stamps Scholarship рассматривает только Early Action / Early Decision applicants в некоторых вузах. Проверить на сайте конкретного вуза.

**Timeline:**
- Application deadline: 1 ноября
- Stamps nomination: automatic (в некоторых вузах) или separate application

### Шаг 2: Выделиться в application

**Common App — Activities Section:**
```
Activity 1: Software Engineer, T-Bank (Professional)
- "Built cashback targeting system processing 30M transactions/day, 
  $600K+ annual impact. Led ClickHouse migration (5x speedup). 
  40+ hours/month saved through automation."
- Hours/week: 40, Weeks/year: 52

Activity 2: Open-Source Maintainer (Community)
- "Created ARC (local-first AI agent platform) and ProofForge 
  (employee accountability platform). 200+ GitHub stars combined. 
  Reduced AI context load by 48%."
- Hours/week: 15, Weeks/year: 40

Activity 3: Telegram Mini App Developer (Entrepreneurial)
- "Built Svobodno (URVI) — last-minute booking app. Onboarded 3 
  major partners, 35% vacancy reduction, 25% conversion increase."
- Hours/week: 10, Weeks/year: 30
```

**Additional Information Section (optional):**
- Upload 1-page project summary
- Include architecture diagram of ClickHouse migration
- Include GitHub contribution graph

### Шаг 3: Написать exceptional essays

**Common App Essay — Prompt 6 (intellectual curiosity):**
```
"At 2 AM, staring at a query that had been running for 45 minutes, 
I realized my self-taught knowledge had reached its limit. I had 
built a system processing millions of transactions daily, optimized 
ClickHouse queries for 5x speedups, and created AI tools that saved 
hundreds of engineering hours. But I couldn't explain why my 
optimization worked at the algorithmic level.

That night, I opened CLRS 'Introduction to Algorithms' for the 
first time. The chapter on B-trees explained exactly why my 
ClickHouse partitioning strategy worked. It wasn't intuition — 
it was computer science. I spent the next 6 months self-studying 
algorithms, MIT OCW courses, and Harvard CS50.

My work at T-Bank taught me to build. My open-source projects 
taught me to share. But formal education will teach me to 
understand. That's why I'm applying to [University] — to bridge 
the gap between practice and theory, between building systems and 
understanding why they work."
```

### Шаг 4: Получить outstanding recommendations

**For Stamps, recommendations are critical.**

**Letter 1 — Technical Mentor / Tech Lead:**
"Artem is not a typical 20-year-old engineer. He led a ClickHouse 
migration that made our analytics 5x faster, saving $600K+ annually. 
He built AI tools that our entire engineering team now uses. His 
ability to combine system design with business impact is rare even 
among senior engineers."

**Letter 2 — Professor:**
"Despite working full-time, Artem maintained top grades while 
self-studying advanced computer science. His GitHub shows consistent 
contributions to open-source projects that solve real problems. 
He's the kind of student who will raise the bar for everyone in 
class."

**Tips for recommenders:**
- Дать им 1-page summary of achievements
- Попросить сравнить с peers ("top 1% I've worked with")
- Попросить упомянуть leadership, initiative, impact

### Шаг 5: Пройти interview (if shortlisted)

**Stamps finalists обычно interview с:**
- University admissions committee
- Stamps Foundation representatives
- Current Stamps Scholars

**Interview prep:**
- "Tell me about yourself" → 2-minute story combining engineering + motivation
- "Why this university?" → Specific professors, labs, courses
- "How will you contribute to campus?" → Hackathon organizer, open-source club, mentor
- "Tell me about a failure" → Pick a technical failure and how you fixed it
- "What do you want to do in 10 years?" → Ambitious but realistic ("build distributed AI infrastructure")

**Stamps-specific questions:**
- "How will you use the enrichment fund?" → "Attend NeurIPS/ICML, visit Silicon Valley startups, contribute to research"
- "What does leadership mean to you?" → "Leading by example, building tools that help others succeed"

## Советы для российских граждан

1. **Early Action обязателен:** Stamps не рассматривает Regular Decision в некоторых вузах.
2. **Test scores:** Submit SAT/ACT если выше 1500/33. Если ниже — test-optional.
3. **Financial documentation:** Даже с full-ride нужно показать proof of funds для I-20 (travel, personal expenses).
4. **Визовый interview:** "Full scholarship" = strong tie to returning home (или explain your plans clearly).
5. **English proficiency:** Duolingo 140+ = excellent for Stamps schools.
6. **Multiple applications:** Подать на Stamps в 3-5 partner universities. Каждый выбирает independently.
