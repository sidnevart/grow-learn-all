# Сценарий: Гражданин России — специфические вопросы

> **Для кого:** Держатели российских паспортов, поступающих в зарубежные вузы
> **Статус:** Российский паспорт, возможно — прописка в РФ
> **Цель:** Поступить и получить визу в US/Europe

---

## 1. Визовые вопросы

### United States — F-1 Student Visa

**Текущая ситуация (2026):**
- Российские граждане **не запрещены** от получения F-1 visa
- **НО:** US Embassy в Москве **прекратила routine visa services** в 2022
- Визу нужно получать в third-country embassies

#### Third-Country Options (ранжировано по доступности):

| Город | Страна | Плюсы | Минусы | Как записаться |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|---------------|
| **Almaty** | Казахстан | Близко, много слотов, русскоязычная среда | Нужна виза Казахстана | travel.state.gov |
| **Yerevan** | Армения | Близко, русскоязычная среда, slots есть | Меньше слотов чем Almaty | travel.state.gov |
| **Tbilisi** | Грузия | Близко, no visa for Russians | Slots ограничены | travel.state.gov |
| **Istanbul** | Турция | Большое консульство | Очень busy, long wait times | travel.state.gov |
| **Belgrade** | Сербия | No visa for Russians | Дороже чем Central Asia | travel.state.gov |
| **Warsaw** | Польша | EU quality | Нужна Schengen visa | travel.state.gov |
| **Dubai** | UAE | Много slots | Очень дорого | travel.state.gov |

#### Как записаться на interview:

1. **Fill DS-160** online: [ceac.state.gov/genniv](https://ceac.state.gov/genniv)
2. **Pay MRV fee** ($185) — через bank transfer или card
   - **Проблема для россиян:** Sanctions may block Russian cards
   - **Решение:** Pay through third-country bank, or ask friend/relative abroad
3. **Create profile** on [ustraveldocs.com](https://ustraveldocs.com)
4. **Select embassy location** (Almaty, Yerevan, etc.)
5. **Schedule interview** — slots often book months ahead
6. **Pay SEVIS fee** ($350) — i901fee.com

#### Required documents for interview:

| Документ | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Passport | Valid 6+ months beyond program end |
| Form I-20 | From university (after acceptance + deposit) |
| DS-160 confirmation | Print with barcode |
| MRV fee receipt | $185 |
| SEVIS fee receipt | $350 |
| Photo | US visa specification (5x5 cm, white background) |
| Bank statements | Proof of $60K-80K/year |
| Scholarship letters | If applicable |
| Transcripts | School + any university |
| Test scores | SAT/TOEFL |
| Acceptance letter | From university |

#### Interview tips for Russians:

**Главный вопрос:** "Will you return to Russia after studies?"

**Хороший ответ:**
```
"I plan to return to Russia after graduation to apply my 
education in the Russian tech sector. I have family, property, 
and professional connections in Moscow. My goal is to bring 
advanced distributed systems knowledge back to Russian 
engineering teams."
```

**Плохой ответ:**
```
"I want to stay in the US and work at Google." → Red flag! 
F-1 is NON-IMMIGRANT visa. Must show intent to return.
```

**Что НЕ говорить:**
- "I want to immigrate"
- "US is better than Russia"
- "I want to work in US after graduation" (OPT — да, но не упоминай на interview)
- Sensitive topics: military service, politics, sanctions

**Что говорить:**
- "I want to learn and return"
- "I have strong ties to Russia" (family, property, job offers)
- "My experience at T-Bank shows I contribute to Russian economy"
- Focus on EDUCATION, not immigration

### Europe — Student Visas

#### Germany

**Visum zu Studienzwecken (Student Visa):**
- Apply at German embassy in Moscow OR third-country
- Processing time: 4-12 weeks
- **Blocked account required:** €11,208 in Sperrkonto

**Blocked Account для россиян:**
- **Проблема:** Sanctions may complicate transfers from Russian banks
- **Решения:**
  1. **Fintiba** (fintiba.com) — online, accepts international transfers
  2. **Expatrio** (expatrio.com) — online
  3. **Transfer through non-Russian bank** — if you have account in Kazakhstan, Armenia, etc.
  4. **Cash deposit** — some providers accept cash (check policy)

**Documents:**
- Passport
- Admission letter
- Blocked account proof
- Health insurance
- Passport photos
- Visa application form
- Proof of language proficiency

#### Netherlands

**MVV (Machtiging tot Voorlopig Verblijf) + Residence Permit:**
- University sponsors the process
- Apply through university after acceptance
- **Proof of funds:** €13,000+/year

**For Russians:**
- Нет специфических ограничений
- Apply at Dutch embassy in Moscow OR third-country
- Processing: 2-4 weeks

#### UK

**Student Route Visa:**
- Apply online: [gov.uk/student-visa](https://gov.uk/student-visa)
- **CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies)** from university
- **Proof of funds:** Tuition + living costs
- **IHS (Immigration Health Surcharge):** £470/year
- **TB test:** Required (Russia is listed country)

**For Russians:**
- Apply through UK Visa Application Centre in Moscow
- Or third-country if Moscow closed
- Processing: 3 weeks (standard), 5 days (priority +£250)

#### Estonia

**D-Visa (Long Stay):**
- Apply at Estonian embassy
- Or through VFS Global
- Processing: 30 days

**For Russians:**
- Russian citizens need visa to enter Estonia
- Student D-visa allows 20 hrs/week work
- After graduation: 1 year job search visa

## 2. Финансовые санкции и оплата

### Проблемы:

| Проблема | Описание | Решение |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **Russian cards blocked internationally** | Visa/Mastercard issued in Russia don't work abroad | Use cards from Kazakh/Armenian/Georgian banks |
| **SWIFT restrictions** | Some Russian banks removed from SWIFT | Use banks not under sanctions (Tinkoff, Сбер — check current status) |
| **Application fees** | Common App ($80), CSS Profile ($25), SAT ($60) | Use non-Russian card, or crypto (rarely accepted), or ask friend to pay |
| **SEVIS fee ($350)** | Must pay for F-1 visa | Use international payment method |
| **University tuition** | Large transfers | Wire transfer through non-sanctioned bank, or use services like Wise |

### Решения:

#### 1. Bank account in neighboring country

**Kazakhstan (easiest for Russians):**
- Open account in Kaspi Bank or Halyk Bank
- Deposit cash or transfer from Russia (still possible)
- Get local debit card (works internationally)
- Visit Almaty for 1-2 days

**Armenia:**
- Open account in Ameria Bank or Ardshinbank
- Similar process
- Yerevan — popular destination for Russians

**Georgia:**
- Open account in TBC Bank or Bank of Georgia
- Tbilisi — easy to visit from Russia

**UAE:**
- Emirates NBD или ADCB
- Dubai — more expensive, but reliable

#### 2. Wise (TransferWise)

- wise.com
- Transfer from Russian card (if not blocked) to foreign account
- Low fees, good rates
- May be restricted for some Russian cards — check

#### 3. Cryptocurrency

- **НЕ для application fees** — вузы не принимают crypto
- **Но:** Can convert crypto to fiat through exchanges
- **Risk:** High volatility

#### 4. Friends / Family abroad

- Ask friend or relative with foreign bank account to pay
- Repay them in Russia (cash or Russian transfer)
- Most reliable method

#### 5. Cash

- Bring cash USD/EUR when traveling
- Deposit in foreign bank account
- Declare if >$10,000 at customs

## 3. Военная служба (для мужчин)

### Обязательная военная служба в России:
- **Срок:** 1 год
- **Возраст:** 18-27 лет (если не получил отсрочку)
- **Отсрочка:** University students get deferment

### Если уходишь учиться за рубеж:

**Вариант 1: Отсрочка через «учебу за рубежом»**
- Раньше: можно было получить отсрочку на время обучения
- Сейчас: **Check current policy** — может быть изменено
- Contact военкомат BEFORE leaving

**Вариант 2: «Учеба в иностранном вузе» как основание**
- Some students successfully get deferment for full degree program
- Need official acceptance letter + proof of enrollment
- Process through военкомат

**Вариант 3: Возвращение на каникулах**
- Some students return during breaks to serve
- Risky — could disrupt studies

**Вариант 4: Проживание за рубежом (если получаешь residency)**
- If you get permanent residency in EU → may be exempt
- But loses Russian citizenship benefits

### Важно:
- **Do NOT ignore военкомат** — can lead to problems when returning
- **Document everything** — save acceptance letters, enrollment confirmations
- **Consult lawyer** if uncertain (many Russian lawyers specialize in this)
- **Check current policy** — rules change frequently

## 4. Апостиль и документы в России

### Апостиль для российских документов:

**Кто выдает:**
- **Образовательные документы:** Министерство просвещения РФ
- **Нотариальные документы:** Министерство юстиции РФ
- **Судебные документы:** Суды / Минюст

**Процесс:**
1. **Original document** (аттестат + приложение)
2. **Submit to Минпросвещения** (or through МФЦ)
3. **Wait 2-4 weeks**
4. **Receive apostilled document**

**Commercial expediting:**
- apostille-msk.ru
- legal-trans.ru
- Стоимость: ~5,000₽ за документ
- Время: 3-5 дней

### Переводы:

**Нотариальный перевод в России:**
- Найти переводчика (нота.ру, perevod.ru)
- Заверить у нотариуса
- Стоимость: 1,500-3,000₽ за документ

**Certified translation abroad:**
- If apostilled document in hand — translate abroad
- Sometimes cheaper
- Check university requirements

## 5. Выезд из России

### Если у тебя паспорт РФ:

**Прямой выезд:**
- Можно лететь напрямую в: Kazakhstan, Armenia, Georgia, Turkey, UAE, Serbia
- Нет запрета на выезд для студентов
- **Exception:** If you have военная обязанность issues — check with военкомат

**Через third country для визы:**
- Fly to Almaty → US embassy
- Fly to Yerevan → US embassy
- Fly to Istanbul → US or EU embassy

### Что взять с собой:

| Документ | Почему |
|----------|--------|
| Passport (РФ) | Для возвращения в Россию |
| University acceptance letter | Для визового interview |
| I-20 / CAS | Для визового interview |
| Bank statements | Proof of funds |
| Transcripts | If additional documents requested |
| SEVIS receipt | For US visa |
| DS-160 confirmation | For US visa |

### Что оставить в России:

- **Property documents** (shows ties to home country)
- **Family contacts** (shows ties)
- **Job contract** (if keeping position)
- **Bank account** (keep active)

## 6. Возвращение в Россию после учебы

### Для визового interview (proof of ties):

**Documents to mention:**
- Family in Russia (parents, siblings)
- Property in Russia
- Job offer to return to (or intent to find one)
- Professional network in Russia
- Open-source projects serving Russian community

**What to say:**
```
"I have strong ties to Russia: my family lives in Moscow, 
I own property, and I have a professional network at T-Bank 
and in the Russian developer community. After graduation, I 
plan to return to Russia and contribute my education to 
the Russian tech sector."
```

### Если захочешь остаться в US/Europe:

**Legal pathways:**
- **US:** OPT (1-3 years work), then H-1B (lottery), then Green Card
- **EU:** Job search visa (1 year), then work permit, then permanent residency
- **UK:** Graduate visa (2 years), then Skilled Worker visa

**But for F-1 interview:** Focus on RETURN, not staying.

## 7. Психологические аспекты

### Если у тебя есть страх/сомнения:

**"Меня не примут из-за паспорта"**
- Факт: Российские студенты поступают в MIT, Stanford, Harvard ежегодно
- Факт: Санкции не касаются academic exchange для студентов
- Факт: Universities separate politics from academics

**"Я потеряю связь с Россией"**
- Russian tech community online (Telegram, Habr) — останешься connected
- Можно вернуться на breaks
- Remote work для Russian companies possible
- Open-source projects — global community

**"Мне будет виновато перед семьей"**
- Frame as investment: "После education я смогу поддерживать семью лучше"
- Many Russian families support education abroad
- Video calls, visits — modern connectivity

## Чеклист для российского паспорта

### Before leaving Russia:
- [ ] Check военкомат status (men 18-27)
- [ ] Get apostille for аттестат
- [ ] Get certified translations
- [ ] Open bank account in Kazakhstan/Armenia/Georgia (recommended)
- [ ] Pay all application fees
- [ ] Book test center appointments (Almaty/Yerevan)
- [ ] Get passport photos (US, UK, EU specs)
- [ ] Scan ALL documents to cloud

### For visa:
- [ ] Book visa interview slot (months ahead!)
- [ ] Prepare financial documents
- [ ] Practice interview answers
- [ ] Book flights to third country
- [ ] Arrange accommodation for visa trip
- [ ] Get travel insurance

### After arrival:
- [ ] Register with university international office
- [ ] Apply for local bank account
- [ ] Get local SIM card
- [ ] Register with immigration (if required)
- [ ] Join Russian student association (if exists)
- [ ] Stay connected with home
