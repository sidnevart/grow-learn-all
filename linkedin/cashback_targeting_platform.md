## LinkedIn Description

Backend platform for partner-funded cashback targeting in banking. The system builds customer audiences from financial behavior, supports partner offer delivery, and runs analytical scenarios over high-volume transaction history.

• Business problem: cashback campaign setup and targeting diagnostics depended on slow legacy workflows, partial manual steps, and large transaction datasets that were hard to query quickly.
• Led backend development in Java, Kotlin, Go, Spring Boot, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, gRPC, and microservices.
• Built spend/income-based targeting over 30M+ transactions/day and 1.5 years of transaction history.
• Migrated critical audience-building workflows to ClickHouse-as-a-Service, delivering a 5x speedup on key analytics scenarios.
• Automated partner region setup, saving 40-60 hours of manual work per month.
• Added early validation for targeting scenarios, making issue diagnosis 20-40 minutes faster.
• Removed 80% of legacy code, achieved full documentation coverage, and improved long-term maintainability.
• Alongside the platform work, improved backend CI/CD reliability and speed: 30% faster pipeline execution, 20 minutes saved per run, and all flaky tests eliminated.
• Result: platform improvements contributed to $600K+ annual business impact while making cashback targeting faster, more reliable, and easier to operate.
