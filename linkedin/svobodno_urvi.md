## LinkedIn Description

Telegram Mini App marketplace for discounted last-minute service slots. Customers discover hot slots, partners publish availability, and admins manage marketplace operations.

• Business problem: service businesses had unused appointment slots, while customers wanted spontaneous bookings and better prices.
• Built an MVP three-sided marketplace for customers, partners, and admins.
• Implemented customer discovery and booking flows for last-minute service slots across categories like beauty, car service, restaurants, and leisure.
• Built partner onboarding and YClients integration so businesses could connect existing schedules and publish available slots.
• Developed backend and frontend using Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, React, Vite, Tailwind, Telegram Mini App SDK, and YClients API.
• Onboarded 3 major partners, including DoubleKiss.
• AI partner support reduced slot vacancy by 35%, smart pricing recommendations increased partner revenue by 20%, and the internal recommendation engine improved booking conversion by 25%.
