## LinkedIn Description

AI-powered virtual office for distributed work, business research, opportunity analysis, and agent-driven development workflows. Built around OpenClaw with monitoring, transparent agent execution, and workflow automation.

• Business problem: distributed teams lacked persistent shared workspace, while research, weekly reporting, opportunity tracking, and development coordination required repeated manual effort.
• Built a virtual workspace with desk, meeting, hot desk, and lounge zones, plus real-time presence and AI-assisted workflows.
• Integrated AI weekly reports, business research, VC deal discovery, opportunity analysis, and project execution pipelines.
• Configured OpenClaw-based agents that could take high-level task descriptions, plan implementation, execute development steps, and surface progress through UI/monitoring.
• Added Grafana monitoring and token-usage visibility to make agent work observable instead of opaque.
• Optimized agent workflows and token economy, reducing token cost by 60%.
• Result: 40% engagement lift, 8+ hours of manual analysis saved per week, and 60% token cost reduction.
