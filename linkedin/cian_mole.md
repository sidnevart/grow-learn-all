## LinkedIn Description

Commercial real estate analytics platform that combines auction data, CIAN listings, geocoding, Telegram bot access, admin tools, and a searchable object catalog.

• Business problem: realtors spent hours manually collecting listings, comparing nearby properties, and calculating sale/rental economics from fragmented sources.
• Built backend services with Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL, Redis, Telegram Bot API, geocoding, and data collection pipelines.
• Integrated CIAN listings, auction data, geocoding, maps, and structured object search into one workflow.
• Built a Telegram bot for fast mobile access and an admin panel for managing objects, data quality, and analysis results.
• Designed economic analysis flows for sale/rent profitability, cash flow, yield, payback period, and district-level market comparison.
• Made analytics work by region, street, or district, enabling scalable nationwide usage across Russian commercial real estate markets.
• Result: property economics evaluation in about 10 minutes, district market analysis in 7-10 minutes, and 90% less manual data collection.
